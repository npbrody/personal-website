Why we need statistics: sampling error

Each dot is a sample mean. The bars show the range where the true population mean plausibly sits. Change the sample sizes and watch what happens to the bars.

Quick settings:
GroupnMeanSDSE (SD ÷ √n)Mean ± 1 SE95% interval

Intervals can overlap a little and the difference can still be significant, so the test, not the picture, makes the call. This is an independent-samples t-test with pooled variance (the "equal variances assumed" row in SPSS), and the p value is two-tailed.
