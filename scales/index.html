SCALES & INSTRUMENTS

Interactive versions of survey instruments I use in research and teaching. Each one scores your answers and, where published norms exist, shows how your results compare.

  • Love Styles Quiz

    What you look for in a romantic partner, across six love styles: Eros, Ludus, Storge, Pragma, Mania, and Agape.

    Adapted from Hendrick, C., & Hendrick, S. (1990). A relationship-specific version of the Love Attitudes Scale. Journal of Social Behavior and Personality, 5, 239–254.

    Take the quiz →

  • Relational Development Skills Assessment

    Five domains of interpersonal competence: initiation, negative assertion, self-disclosure, emotional support, and conflict management.

    Buhrmester, D., Furman, W., Wittenberg, M. T., & Reis, H. T. (1988). Five domains of interpersonal competence in peer relationships. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 55(6), 991–1008. Reproduced/adapted with permission of the American Psychological Association.

    Take the quiz →

  • Communication Anxiety Assessment

    The PRCA-24, measuring communication apprehension overall and across group discussion, meetings, interpersonal conversation, and public speaking. Compares your scores to published college-student norms.

    McCroskey, J. C. (1982). An introduction to rhetorical communication (4th ed.). Englewood Cliffs, NJ: Prentice-Hall.

    Take the quiz →

  • Social Media Mindsets Quiz

    Measures valence (whether social media use feels positive or like a waste of time) and agency (how much control you feel over your own use), and compares your scores to a university student sample.

    Based on Lee, A. Y., & Hancock, J. T. (2024). Social media mindsets: a new approach to understanding social media use and psychological well-being. Journal of Computer-Mediated Communication, 29(1), zmad048.

    Take the quiz →

  • Barcelona Music Reward Questionnaire

    Discover your music personality: how you experience reward from music across five facets, including emotion, mood regulation, and sensory-motor response.

    Mas-Herrero, E., Marco-Pallarés, J., Lorenzo-Seva, U., Zatorre, R. J., & Rodriguez-Fornells, A. (2013). Individual differences in music reward experiences. Music Perception, 31(2), 118–138.

    Take the quiz →
